Reading the sticks Mode 2

Control inputs are described as a pair of sticks. Learn the four axes here once.

Left stick

Up / down — Throttle. Up adds power and lift; down cuts it.

Left / right — Yaw. Rotates the drone left or right in place.

Right stick

Up / down — Pitch. Up drops the nose to fly forward; down raises it.

Left / right — Roll. Tips the drone onto its left or right side.

Safety & where to fly

A starting orientation, not legal advice
Know the rules

In the U.S., recreational flight follows the FAA's rules for recreational flyers; flying for any non-recreational purpose requires a Part 107 certificate. Pass the free TRUST test, register your drone if it's over 250 g, and check for a Remote ID requirement.

Altitude & airspace

Stay at or below 400 ft AGL and clear of controlled airspace unless you have authorization (LAANC). Use a current airspace map before you fly somewhere new.

Line of sight & spotter

Keep the aircraft in visual line of sight, with a visual observer if you're in goggles. Many parks and events require this.

Where to fly

Pick open spaces away from people, roads, and airports: fields, sanctioned FPV spots, and clubs (AMA fields). Some state parks require a permit or ban drones outright, so check before you go, and get landowner permission on private property.

Fly within your skill

Give yourself altitude to recover, drop your rates when learning, and practice new tricks in a simulator first. Every sim crash is a free lesson.

Rules change and vary by location. Always confirm the current regulations for where you fly.

Import & export

Your mastery stars and theme live in this browser's storage. Clearing site data wipes it, Safari drops it after about a week without a visit, and it does not follow you to another browser, device, or domain. Export writes it all to one file you can keep or carry across. Each export is stamped with the date and time it was taken, so backups pile up in order instead of overwriting each other. Importing merges into what is here already, keeping the higher star level for any trick in both, so restoring an old backup never costs you progress.
